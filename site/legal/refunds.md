# Refund Policy

Etude is free to use without paying, so you can try everything before you subscribe or buy. Because of that, payments are final, with the exceptions below.

## Etude Sync

- **No refunds for subscription periods**, including partly used ones. Cancel any time and Sync keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for. Nothing on your devices is ever deleted.
- **We refund when the mistake is ours:** a duplicate charge, a charge after you cancelled, or a billing error.
- **Outages:** if Sync is unavailable for more than 7 days in a row because of a problem on our side, we refund that billing period.
- Sync is sold by Polar as merchant of record, so refunds are issued through Polar to your original payment method. Write to hello@etude.study or reply to your Polar receipt.

## Paid Market decks

- **No refunds** once a deck is in your library, except:
  - the deck doesn't work in Etude, or is materially different from its description and preview, **and** you report it within 14 days of purchase **and** it isn't fixed within 7 days of your report;
  - you were charged twice for the same deck.
- Purchases from outside the regions where the paid Market is open (currently Canada and the United States) are refunded automatically.
- If you're in the EU or UK, at checkout you're asked to agree to immediate access to the deck, which ends the 14-day withdrawal right that otherwise applies to digital content.
- A refunded deck is removed from your library.

## Where the law says otherwise

Nothing here limits a refund right that the consumer law where you live gives you and that can't be waived.

## Chargebacks

Please write to us before disputing a charge with your bank. If the charge was our mistake, we'll refund it faster than a dispute would. A disputed purchase is removed from your library while the dispute is open.
