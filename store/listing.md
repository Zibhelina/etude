## Description (Apple 4000 max, Play full description 4000 max)

```
Etude is a free spaced-repetition app where a flashcard can be more than text on two sides.

A card can be a small interactive app: tap a country on a real world map, find an element on a blank periodic table, tap out a letter in Morse code, drag events onto a timeline, play a chord on a piano, or work out a sum with fresh numbers every time you see it. Etude checks your answer and tells you what you missed.

FSRS SCHEDULING
Etude uses FSRS, a modern spaced-repetition algorithm, to show each card again just before you'd forget it. Set an exam date on a deck and Etude makes sure everything gets reviewed before the day. Stats show how much you remember now and what's about to slip.

FREE DECKS
The Market has free decks, card types and themes made and shared by other people: countries of the world, the periodic table, Morse code, logic puzzles and more. Install one in a tap and start practising.

MAKE YOUR OWN
Write your own cards, organise them into decks and subdecks, and import Anki decks. Card types are HTML and JavaScript, so if you can build it in a web page you can practise it in Etude.

AI GRADING, IF YOU WANT IT
For open answers you can turn on AI grading with your own OpenAI or OpenRouter key. Your answers go straight from your device to the provider you choose. It's off until you set it up.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your collection lives on your device. Etude has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and you don't need an account. An optional account lets you keep several devices in step with end-to-end encryption: your collection is locked on your device before it leaves, and we can't read it.

Etude is open source. Questions or ideas: hello@etude.study
```
